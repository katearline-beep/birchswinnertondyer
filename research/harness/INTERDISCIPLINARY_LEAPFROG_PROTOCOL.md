## Decision protocol for EVERY proposed cross-disciplinary leapfrog

1. **Type the object:** field, prime, curve, coefficients, lattice, Selmer local conditions, filtration, and base vs tower.
2. **State the exact theorem or conjecture** and verify every hypothesis, especially whether a claim concerns complex or p-adic L-functions.
3. **Expose the bridge** as a diagram or named map, with kernel/cokernel and compatibility; do not infer an arrow from analogous vocabulary.
4. **Demand a discriminating test** with a concrete negative control and independent implementation when arithmetic is involved.
5. **Rank by decisive leverage:** prioritize an exact nonzero second-height entry after certified h1/Selmer, not more digits of a universal norm; maintain a separate original-complex comparison route.
6. **Review full BSD scope:** original L'', analytic rank, Sha, leading coefficient, general theorem. No finite test can silently discharge these.
