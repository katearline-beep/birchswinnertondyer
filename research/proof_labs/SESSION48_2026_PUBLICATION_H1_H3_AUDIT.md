## Publications examined, theorem type and precise stop gates

**1. Bullach–Burns, _On Euler systems and Nekovář–Selmer complexes_, arXiv:2509.13894v2, revised 2026-04-01.**
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13894v2, Introduction §§1, 3–7, Theorems 4.20, 5.29, 6.38, Cor. 9.7 and Remark 9.8. They develop Euler/Kolyvagin and higher Stark systems directly over Nekovář–Selmer complexes with local complete Gorenstein coefficient rings, explicitly accommodating Greenberg local conditions. Their generalized formalism is the **best H1/H2 engineering/mathematical template**, in particular the defining Selmer cone, duality, local homotopies, relative core vertices, residual-triviality and error terms. The introductory concrete elliptic curve Kato main-conjecture theorem, however, *explicitly assumes L(E/K,1)≠0 and integral Galois image containing SL₂(Z_p)* (and further p-local conditions). We cannot invoke that rank-zero conclusion for our rank-four curve. Their general machinery requires suitable Euler-system inputs and source-matched exact local conditions; they do **not** construct our c1/c2 or assert the original complex L'' vanishes. **Accept as framework; do not apply rank-zero elliptic corollary.** Also note this is v2 of a 2025/26 preprint, not a November release.

**2. Ray–Sujatha, _Massey products and the Iwasawa theory of fine Selmer groups_, Canadian Journal of Mathematics, published online 2026-10-16, DOI 10.4153/S0008414X26102478.**
Source: https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X26102478 (Cambridge full HTML). Section 6, **Theorem 6.9** (citing Lam–Liu–Sharifi–Wake–Wang) identifies the image, under a defined projection p_{φ,θ}, of a generalized Bockstein Ψ^(n)([f]) with a Massey product for a proper defining system. **The theorem itself warns the projection need not determine the whole Bockstein image.** Theorem **7.1** gives an equivalence between fine Selmer cotorsion/μ=0 and spanning by higher Massey products, assuming v|p totally ramified and all S primes finitely decomposed in the chosen Z_p tower. The p-adic *fine Selmer* condition is NOT our ordinary **Greenberg Selmer** condition at the two split 11-adic places. Its Galois representation/coefficient and filtration must also be matched exactly. **Accept as explicit finite-order Bockstein/Massey method, NOT as a theorem proving our second supported obstruction vanishes or a Greenberg comparison.** Verify both local decomposition hypotheses, which were NOT audited here.
