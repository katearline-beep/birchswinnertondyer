## C-0036: why the natural finite quotient cannot contain the lift

### Arithmetic image input, with review boundary

For this subsection use the recorded C0007/20 residual-image argument,
subject to its outstanding expert review: split multiplicative I1 at117223
supplies a nontrivial transvection in rho(G_K). Frobenius23 has irreducible

the mod11 cyclotomic character, surjective over K because K=Q(sqrt(-7))
is disjoint from Q(zeta11), whose quadratic subfield is Q(sqrt(-11)).
Thus G=rho(G_K)=GL2(F11). Only this residual statement is used; there is
no inference from finite code to the full Z11 image. Local Tate-uniformization
and finite root-generation facts are trusted standard source inputs, as
in [the existing audit](../strategy/KATO_REAL11_LOCAL_IMAGE_AUDIT.md).

Write V=E[11], L=K(E[11]), and let F be the Galois field obtained by
adjoining all five11-division torsors to L. Its group embeds in
V^5 semidirect G; W=Gal(F/L) is a G-submodule of V^5.

The central scalar−I shows H1(G,V)=0: if c is a cocycle and s=−I, comparing
c(sg) with c(gs) gives−2c(g)=(rho(g)−1)c(s), so c is a coboundary.
This is Sah's elementary central-scalar argument; compare
[Lombardo–Tronto, *Explicit Kummer Theory for Elliptic Curves*, §4.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.05376).
No expert review is implied by citing it.

The G-action on V is absolutely irreducible, and its matrix span is
Mat2(F11). If W were proper in V^5, a nonzero scalar row λ would annihilate
W: elementary upper/lower matrices show a submodule here has form
V tensor L0 for a subspace L0 of F11^5. Then Σ λi*c_Pi restricts to zero
on G_L. Inflation–restriction and H1(G,V)=0 would make that global Kummer
class zero, contradicting C0035. Hence W=V^5 and

\[
 \operatorname{Gal}(F/K)=V^5\rtimes G,\quad
 [F:K]=11^{10}\cdot13200=342374004733200.
\]

This uses a special isotypic-module argument, **not** Maschke's theorem:
11 divides #G. A single nonzero residual combination similarly has Kummer
splitting field degree11²*13200=1597200. These degrees are deduced from the
image hypothesis and saturation certificate, **not independently computed
splitting fields**.

For a lift of the central scalar−I, commutators with W multiply W by−2.
Thus W lies in the commutator subgroup. GL2(F11)'s abelianization is its
determinant group F11*, of order10, since its elementary subgroup SL2(F11)

\[
 \operatorname{Gal}(FD_1/K)=H\times C_{11},\qquad H=\operatorname{Gal}(F/K).

### A short cochain proof, not a huge group calculation

This lemma holds for ANY H-module V and nonzero class [c0] in H1(H,V).
Extend rho,c0 trivially across C11, and let k be its character, with
k(tau)=1. A first-order lift under the action
rho(g)*(1+k(g)T) would require

\[
 c_1(gh)=c_1(g)+\rho(g)c_1(h)+k(g)\rho(g)c_0(h).
\]

In H×C11, tau commutes with every h in H. Computing c1(tau*h) and
c1(h*tau) therefore forces

\[
 c_0(h)=(\rho(h)-1)c_1(\tau),
\]

which makes c0 a coboundary, contradiction. Negating k for the inverse
Iwasawa action changes the sign and leaves the contradiction unchanged.
Therefore **no nonzero residual combination of the five Kummer classes
has a first-order lift factoring through this natural finite quotient.**
The result is a conditional arithmetic no-go using the stated image input;
C0035's reduction certificate itself does not depend on that input.

Crucially, inflation on H2 need NOT be injective. The obstruction can vanish
on G_K while remaining nonzero on this finite quotient. Thus we have NOT
shown that the actual first Selmer lift is impossible. Nor have we disproved
C0032's rational S2 statement. Rational membership alone does not dispose of
integral obstruction torsion; a mod11 lift still needs its own argument.
This corrects a possible *future implementation strategy*, not an earlier
claim that an actual lift had already been computed.

## Source scope and full-problem step back

Lam–Liu–Sharifi–Wake–Wang,
[*Generalized Bockstein maps and Massey products*, Theorem4.3.1 and §4.3](https://wangjiuya.github.io/research/augmentation.pdf),
identify the procyclic generalized Bockstein with a Massey product relative
to a **proper defining system**; the coefficient module may carry a G-action.
For R=F11, quotient H=C11 and truncation n=2, their coefficient-size condition
holds. This supplies a source-level global cohomology bridge once the lift
is supplied. It does not create that lift or its Greenberg local homotopies.
The scalar absolute-Galois Massey-vanishing theorem is not a theorem that
our twisted Selmer second height is zero: coefficients, defining systems
and local conditions differ. Its use as an automatic shortcut is rejected.

For H3 we rechecked Yun–Zhang,
[*Shtukas and the Taylor expansion of L-functions (II)*, Annals2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.08026).
Their higher-derivative products and Heegner–Drinfeld intersections are over
**function fields in odd characteristic**. The r=3,a=2 derivative-product
type resembles the desired L''(E,1)*L'(E^-7,1) bridge, but no characteristic-zero
cycle, comparison map or independent intersection vanishing is supplied.
This is a precisely scoped inspiration, not a theorem about E/Q. H3 remains
open; H4/H6 remain parked and H5 diagnostic. No new original complex BSD
implication follows from C0035 or C0036.
