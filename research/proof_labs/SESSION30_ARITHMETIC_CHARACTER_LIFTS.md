## C-0033: an actual Artin character and compatible arithmetic layers

Put omega=(1+sqrt(-7))/2. Since O_K has class number1 and units ±1,
its ring-class group at conductor f=11^(m+1) is the split residue ratio
(O_K/f)^*/(Z/f)^* = (Z/f)^*. Taking the 11-primary quotient gives C_(11^m).
These are the anticyclotomic layers: conjugation inverts the ratio. This
identification uses the ring-class exact sequence and global class field
theory, not a conclusion of a finite matrix test. See the existing Session23
source audit and [Sutherland, Theorem21.1](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.783/2022/LectureNotes21.pdf).

Choose the root omega=5 mod11; its lifts are16 mod121 and137 mod1331.
For a principal ideal (a+b*omega) prime to11, write

\[
 r_m(a,b)=\frac{a+b\omega_+}{a+b\omega_-}\pmod {11^{m+1}},\qquad
 \ell_m((a+b\omega))=\log_{12}(r_m(a,b)^{10})\pmod {11^m}.
\]

Here log_12 means the **exact discrete logarithm** in the principal-unit
cyclic group generated by12, not a floating logarithm. This fixes the Artin
coordinate, and the generator gamma is chosen accordingly. Arithmetic
Frobenius is used, calibrated against PARI's ideal Artin quotient. An inverse
Artin convention negates ell; the Iwasawa coefficient action must record its
own inversion separately. Local reciprocity at a ramified place is not
inferred by calling its uniformizer an unramified Frobenius.

At m=1 the character is the Fermat quotient (r^10-1)/11 mod11. At m=2 it is
**not** the uncorrected quotient modulo121: use log(1+11z)/log(12), retaining
the quadratic logarithm term. Enumeration of powers and an independent
truncated logarithm formula agree on all110 and1210 possible ratios.

[PARI oracle](pari_anticyclotomic_artin_t3.gp): bnfcertify=1; ray groups
C110×C55 and C1210×C605; kill their rational diagonal and take the primary
quotient. The resulting subgroups have indices11 and121, exact conductors
121*O_K and1331*O_K, and conjugation acts by−1. All **278 prime-ideal Artin
values**, over rational primes≤500 except11 in both layers, agree with the
[separate Python calculation](anticyclotomic_artin_t3.py).

The first layer is K times the field defined by the explicit polynomial

\[
\begin{aligned}

## Why two layers matter for the second Bockstein

For coefficients mod11^m and truncation through T² the action is

\[
 (1+T)^k=1+kT+\binom{k}{2}T^2\pmod{11^m,T^3}.
\]

All pairs k,l mod11^m satisfy the multiplication law in the executable.
Omitting the binomial term fails already for k=l=1. The norm/sum character
is cyclotomic and fails conjugation inversion; rational ideal(2) is a
specific negative control. Changing the chosen prime over11 inverts ell.

Over Z/121 the first finite layer C11 imposes

\[
 (1+T)^{11}-1=11T+55T^2=0\pmod{T^3}.
\]

Multiplication by T falsely kills **11T²**, which is nonzero in the free
truncated tower coefficient module. C121 has (1+T)^121-1=0 automatically
modulo(121,T³), so it retains the intended free truncated action. This is
an arithmetic layer-compatibility check, not a higher-height valuation.
No finite layer is substituted for the integral Z11 tower itself.

For the standard inverse Iwasawa action put k(g)=−ell(g), and let rho be
the **elliptic** coefficient action. The global inhomogeneous differential
on c=c0+Tc1+T²c2 has coefficients

\[
\begin{split}
 d_0c_0(g,h)&=\rho(g)c_0(h)-c_0(gh)+c_0(g),\\
 d_0c_1(g,h)&=-k(g)\rho(g)c_0(h),\\
 O_2(g,h)&=\binom{k(g)}2\rho(g)c_0(h)+k(g)\rho(g)c_1(h).
\end{split}
\]

The obstruction is the cohomology class of O2 modulo the appropriate
first-order indeterminacy, with the **local Selmer cone data included**.
These formulas name the required arithmetic input; ell by itself supplies
neither rho nor c0,c1. A local Greenberg null-homotopy cannot be omitted.

## C-0034: five concrete division-point torsors

On the exact short model w²=C(z)=z³−1267z+17230, construct the standard
11-division polynomial psi (degree60, leading coefficient11) and numerator
phi (monic degree121) with z([11]Q)=phi(z(Q))/psi(z(Q))². The new
[FLINT implementation](kummer_11_torsors.py) uses independent normalized
odd/even recurrences. Every coefficient of phi, psi and psi² agrees with
PARI ellxn/elldivpol, not merely the degrees or a few evaluations; see the
[official elliptic documentation](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/dochtml/html/Elliptic_curves.html).

For P=(u,v), u=n/d in lowest terms, set

\[
 F_P(Z)=d\phi(Z)-n\psi(Z)^2,\quad
 A_P=K[Z]/F_P(Z),\quad
 H=\phi'\psi-2\phi\psi'.
\]

This is an **etale algebra of dimension121**, not necessarily a degree121
field. In A_P define the actual point

\[
 Q_P=\left(Z,\;11v\psi(Z)^3/H(Z)\right).
\]

The executable computes a polynomial inverse of H modulo F_P and verifies
Q_P lies on E and [11]Q_P=P. The y formula follows from
[11]^*(dz/(2w))=11 dz/(2w). It checks gcd(F_P,F'_P)=gcd(F_P,H)=1 and the
exact cleared identity

\[
 121v^2\psi^6-C(Z)H^2=0\pmod{F_P}.
\]

It does this separately for the four rational points (15,40),(19,4),
(23,16),(31,88), and R=(-3355/63,(128780/1323)sqrt(-7)); the last y² is
negative rational, while its y belongs to K. Both the y square and its
actual signed square root v must be retained when specifying the point.
A changed input point is refused, and changing phi to phi+1 breaks the
on-curve identity on an otherwise genuine input fibre.

Thus [11]^{-1}(P) is supplied as an explicit E[11]-torsor. Choosing an
embedding of A_P into Kbar gives a division point and the genuine Kummer
cocycle c_P(g)=gQ_P−Q_P; another choice changes it by a coboundary.
**We have not evaluated these cocycles on a Galois presentation.** The
polynomial describes the torsor, not a computed Galois action matrix.
The checked hashes of the five F_P polynomials (ascending coefficients,
comma-separated exact integers) are printed for reproducibility.

Under C0032's accepted exact height and twist inputs, reducing the integral
height coefficients gives residual Kummer classes c_Pj−r_j*c_P1 with
r_j=(2,7,8) mod11. This specifies their classes in H1(K,E[11]); it is
**not** a first-order lift. Rational S2 membership does not automatically
remove integral obstruction torsion: supply a saturation/torsion argument
or work with rational coefficients before claiming an integral lift.
The [11]-torsors do not supply E[121] cocycles either. The richer Artin
character alone cannot upgrade the elliptic precision.
