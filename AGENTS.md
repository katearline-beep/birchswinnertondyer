# Research-agent working contract

**Read this before every research task and PR.** These are repo-level instructions for AI agents and human contributors; they cannot override mathematical evidence or independent review.

1. **Re-establish the goal.** Read `README.md`, `CLAIMS.md`, `RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md`, `research/INTERDISCIPLINARY_TRANSFER_AUDIT.md`, and `research/harness/README.md`. Check current issues and CI status. Never infer that a prior chat or an old commit proved a claim.
2. **Design a leapfrog conjecture before routine calculations.** Apply [LCP-1](research/strategy/LEAPFROG_PROTOCOL.md): name the bottleneck, identify the existing theorem's *typed output*, formulate a speculative IF/THEN bridge, document how it could fail, state the gain if valid, and compare one safe lemma, one credible moonshot, and one wild option. Register each high-risk track in [leapfrogs.json](research/harness/leapfrogs.json); missing arrows and parked ideas are legitimate results. Then choose one falsifiable question.
3. **Choose one falsifiable question.** State precise objects, field, model, primes, mathematical dependency graph, and pass/fail criterion *before* calculating. Record it in the machine-checked harness manifest.
4. **Audit other disciplines as candidate techniques.** For every proposed transfer from physics, biology, coding theory, control theory, formal methods, validated numerics, topology, optimization or elsewhere, explicitly label **exact mathematical machinery** versus **analogy/heuristic**. Give the translation and hypotheses. Use only what improves a proof, counterexample, algorithm, or validation strategy. Analogies never upgrade a claim.
5. **Seek the cheapest decisive experiment.** Prefer a calculation that falsifies an assumption or distinguishes two models over incrementing precision without a question. Include a deliberately failing negative control and a reproducible command wherever practical.
6. **Use orthogonal corroboration.** Verify inputs and outputs with a different mathematical implementation or library (PARI/GP, SageMath, FLINT/Arb, Lean) when possible. Record possible shared underlying assumptions; multiple AI implementations are not human peer review.
7. **Require integral, tower-compatible evidence before upgrading p-adic filtration claims.** A calculation modulo 11 can falsely exhibit deep augmentation vanishing even when the integral element is not in I. For cyclic 11-power fields, test I³/I⁴ at two successive finite layers or explain why the infinite-level object is correctly identified. See [cyclotomic integral counterexamples](research/strategy/CYCLOTOMIC_KATO_FILTRATION_GATE.md).
8. **Be explicit about trusted mathematical dependencies.** Passing code checks only its own assertions and implementation. It cannot establish a modularity theorem, Selmer completeness, a functional equation, Sha finiteness, or BSD. Mark every unproven dependency.
9. **Make claims conditional by default.** Specify: exact statement, claim category, reviewed/unreviewed status, proof obligations, nonclaims, historical corrections, and citations. No claim of novelty or mathematical peer review without evidence.
10. **Document provenance.** Reference the exact commit and original source hashes where available; preserve failed experiments. Do not silently change research claims or CI checks to make them pass.
11. **Use narrow PRs.** Update the relevant harness experiment and claim ledger. Run `python3 -I -B research/harness/validate.py` and `python3 -I -B research/harness/test_validate.py` and the existing math tests. Do not merge while required checks fail.
12. **Escalate appropriately.** When a potential theorem emerges, ask for arithmetic-geometry review with a precise lemma and reproducible counterexample/test, not a broad claim that BSD is solved. Do not contact upstream maintainers with speculative theorem claims.

**Current focal problem:** E/Q of conductor 234446, K=Q(√−7), p=11. Our two-place local logarithm kernel is **not** the one-prime modified Selmer group or the radical of an anticyclotomic derived-height pairing. The `L'(E^{-7},1)` candidate rational enclosure is still subject to a complete mathematical assumptions audit.

If new research uncovers an error, open a correction issue and downgrade or withdraw the affected claim. Research integrity outranks throughput.
